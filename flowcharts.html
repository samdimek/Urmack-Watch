Flow 1 — Evaluation pipeline (per tick)

Runs once per cadence interval per invariant plan. One in-flight evaluation per plan (Redis lock). Breach alerts wait for chain finality depth; sub-depth violations are recorded as pending.

service step engine step decision

Flow 2 — Protocol onboarding (self-serve, Beta target < 30 min)

The dry-run gate is non-negotiable: a spec that fails against 7 days of history cannot be armed. This is what keeps false positives out of the channel on day one.

Flow 3 — Alert lifecycle

Statuses are explicit and auditable. A false positive is not deleted — it becomes spec-tuning signal (loop back to the invariant editor), preserving the honest record.

Flow 4 — Dead-man heartbeat (the monitor watching itself)

A silent monitor is worse than no monitor. Any evaluation missing for 3× its expected interval escalates — first to Urmack ops, then to the client as a "monitor blind" notice with honest wording.

Flow 5 — SDLC roadmap (iterative-incremental, risk-driven)

Each phase ends in a demoable release and a hard gate. Spikes are time-boxed ≤ 2 weeks with explicit pass/fail criteria; a failed spike re-plans the phase.
