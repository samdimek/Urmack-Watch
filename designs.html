First checks (excerpt)

  1. Verify trigger tx is finalized and not reorg-pending.
  2. Compare oracle liabilities vs. pool accounting — stale oracle vs. real shortfall.
  3. Check liquidationBatch parameters — partial liquidation ordering bug pattern (ref: Urmack research L-07).
  4. If shortfall > 5% for 2+ blocks: activate IR retainer bridge.
